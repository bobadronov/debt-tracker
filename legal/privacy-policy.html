DebtTracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

DebtTracker ("the app") is a personal debt-tracking app built by Bohdan Dronov ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and how to have it deleted. If anything here is unclear, contact bobadronov@gmail.com.

Two ways to use the app

DebtTracker works in one of two modes, and it's your choice which one you're in:

  • Local-only (the default): no account, no sign-in. Every debtor, creditor, and transaction you enter is stored only in a local database on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. If you never sign in, we never receive any of your data.
  • Account+Sync (opt-in): you create an account so your data can sync across your own devices in real time. This is where the data described below is collected, and it's the only mode in which your data can reach our servers or another person.

Contact lookup by email or phone

When you add a debtor or creditor and enter an email address or phone number that belongs to another registered DebtTracker account, the app can look up that account's public display name and photo to help you fill in the contact card faster. That lookup only ever returns a name and photo — never anyone's email, password, transactions, or balances.

Sharing a debt with the other person (mirrored debts)

This only happens in Account+Sync mode. If you add a debtor or creditor whose phone number or email address matches another registered DebtTracker user, the app automatically links that debt to their account. There is no separate confirmation step — adding a contact whose details match a registered user is what triggers it.

When a debt is linked:

  • A contact card for you — your profile display name, phone, email, and photo — is created in the other person's account.
  • Every transaction already on that debt (amount, date, payment method, note) is copied into their account, and every transaction you later add or edit on that debt is copied to their mirrored record. Anything they add or edit on their side is copied back to yours the same way.
  • They receive an in-app notification that identifies you by name and shows the amount and currency.

In other words: for a debt that both people track in Account+Sync mode with matching contact details, both people can see that debt's transaction history. Local-only mode never participates in any of this. If you don't want a debt shared, don't enter the other person's registered email or phone number, or track that debt in Local-only mode.

Once data has been copied into the other person's account it belongs to their account. Deleting the debt, deleting all your data, or deleting your account does not remove copies already mirrored to them — the same way you can't take back information you've already shared with someone. The reverse is also true: mirrored copies you received stay in your account until you delete them.

Notifications

When you're signed in, the app checks our notifications table every 15 seconds and shows a system notification for anything new. It does not use Firebase Cloud Messaging, Apple Push Notification service, or any other third-party push provider — there is no push token, and no third party is involved in delivering a notification to your device. You can mark notifications read or delete them in the app; they're deleted with your account.

Sharing a contact by QR code

The QR hub screen can show a QR code containing a small contact card (name, and optionally phone and email) for another DebtTracker user to scan, and can scan someone else's. This is entirely on-device: the code is generated and read locally, and scanning uses your device camera (or, on Desktop/Web, a local image file you pick). Nothing about the QR exchange is sent to our servers. If you're signed in, the card shown is your account profile; if you're not, it's whatever you typed into the "My card" fields, stored only on your device. Adding a scanned contact whose details match a registered user can then trigger a mirrored debt, as described above.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no ad-tracking SDKs.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that phone home to a third party.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.
  • No third-party push-notification service (see "Notifications" above).
  • App-lock (fingerprint/Face ID/PIN) is handled entirely by your device's own operating system. The app only ever receives a yes/no "unlocked" result — it never sees, stores, or transmits any biometric data.

Where data is stored

Account+Sync data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, on servers in the EU. Access to your data is restricted to your own signed-in account by row-level security policies enforced by the database itself — not just by the app. The one exception is a mirrored debt: data you've shared that way is, by design, also readable by the account you shared it with.

The Android and Desktop builds are distributed through Google Play and GitHub Releases; the Android app uses Google Play's in-app update mechanism to tell you when a new version is available. These channels are subject to Google's and GitHub's own privacy policies.

Deleting your data — DebtTracker (developer: Bohdan Dronov)

Option 1 — delete your data, keep your account

In the app, go to Settings → Delete all data and confirm twice. This immediately and permanently deletes every debtor, creditor, and transaction you've synced to our servers. Your account (email/login) stays active so you can keep using the app afterward.

Option 2 — delete your account entirely

Email bobadronov@gmail.com from the address registered to your account with the subject "Delete my account", or simply state your request. We verify the request and delete your account, typically within a few business days, and confirm by reply once it's done.

Children's privacy

DebtTracker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
