DebtTracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

DebtTracker ("the app") is a personal debt-tracking app built by Bohdan Dronov ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and how to have it deleted. If anything here is unclear, contact bobadronov@gmail.com.

Two ways to use the app

DebtTracker works in one of two modes, and it's your choice which one you're in:

  • Local-only (the default): no account, no sign-in. Every debtor, creditor, and transaction you enter is stored only in a local database on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. If you never sign in, we never receive any of your data.
  • Account+Sync (opt-in): you create an account so your data can sync across your own devices in real time. This is where the data described below is collected.

What we collect in Account+Sync mode

DataWhy
Email address & passwordAccount sign-in (handled by our backend provider, Supabase — we never see your password in plain text)
Display name, profile photo (optional)Shown on your own account, and used to auto-fill your name/photo when another DebtTracker user who knows your email adds you as a contact
Names, phone numbers, emails of people you add as debtors/creditorsThe core feature of the app — tracking who owes you and who you owe
Transaction records (amounts, dates, payment method, notes)The core feature of the app
App preferences (theme, language, sound/haptics on/off)Restoring your settings across devices

If a person you add as a debtor/creditor happens to have their own DebtTracker account under the same email, the app can look up their public display name and photo to help you fill in their contact card faster. That lookup only ever returns a name and photo — never anyone's email, password, or financial data — and it never exposes your data to them either.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no ad-tracking SDKs.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that phone home to a third party.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.
  • App-lock (fingerprint/Face ID/PIN) is handled entirely by your device's own operating system. The app only ever receives a yes/no "unlocked" result — it never sees, stores, or transmits any biometric data.

Where data is stored

Account+Sync data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, on servers in the EU. Access to your data is restricted to your own signed-in account by row-level security policies enforced by the database itself — not just by the app.

Deleting your data

In-app: Settings → Delete all data (with a double confirmation) permanently deletes all of your debtors, creditors, and transactions from our servers. Signing out at any time stops syncing; uninstalling the app removes everything stored locally on that device. To delete your account entirely, email bobadronov@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

DebtTracker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change too. We won't reduce your rights over previously collected data without your consent.
